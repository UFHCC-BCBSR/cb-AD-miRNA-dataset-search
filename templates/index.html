Case‑Control RNA‑seq Candidate Search

About

Finds public RNA-seq datasets for case-control comparisons — e.g. Alzheimer's vs. controls in prefrontal cortex. Returns a shortlist of candidate studies to review by hand. Does not download or analyze data.

SRA-first — Broad disease queries against SRA, then filters each run by organism, library strategy, selection, and tissue. Tissue comes from BioSample source_name, since SRA titles rarely mention it.

Literature-first — PubMed search for the condition, then resolves papers to GEO accessions via elink or title match, and reads library prep and sample counts from the GEO record.

Note: The two paths produce separate outputs and are not merged. Case/control group sizes are unreliable in archive metadata — verify against the paper.
